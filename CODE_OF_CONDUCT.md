# Code of conduct

Pulse is built in the open by people with different backgrounds, languages and levels of
experience. This page says how we treat each other in the repository's issues, pull requests,
discussions and any other place that speaks for the project.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in a second language.
- Discuss the work, not the person. Disagree with reasons, and accept that a decision may not go
  your way.
- Assume good intent, and ask before concluding someone meant harm.
- Give credit for other people's work and ideas.
- Keep personal data out of public threads: yours, other people's and your customers'. Remove
  emails, phone numbers, API keys and secrets from logs before posting them.

## What isn't accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats, or demeaning remarks about anyone's identity or background.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Trolling, repeated off-topic posts, or deliberately derailing a discussion.
- Using the project to promote spam or to send people unsolicited messages.

## Reporting

Email **conduct@nivantagroup.com** with what happened, where (links help) and who was involved.
Only the maintainers read it, and we keep the reporter's identity private. You'll get a reply within
a few days. Security problems go through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## What maintainers do

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and other contributions that break this code, and
may warn, temporarily block or permanently ban someone, depending on how serious and how repeated
the behaviour is. We tell the person what was wrong and why, unless that would put the reporter at
risk.

This code applies in all project spaces, and in public spaces when someone is representing the
project.
